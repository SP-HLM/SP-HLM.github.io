THE MOTION INTERFACE

A sparse-pose-driven interface for humanoid loco-manipulation.

Read the abstract +

Humanoid loco-manipulation requires an effective interface between high-level task policies and whole-body control. Existing interfaces typically rely on a single action representation for both task intent and whole-body coordination. This may burden the high-level policy or leave one aspect underspecified. We present SP-HLM, a framework built on a sparse-pose-driven interface. The interface combines five task-critical body poses with a learned latent motion prior to represent spatial intent and whole-body coordination in complementary forms. SP-VLA, a vision-language-action model, first predicts task-relevant sparse poses and finger joint positions from multimodal observations and language instructions using asynchronous action-chunk inference. Given these sparse targets, SP-MGM, a motion generation model trained through two-stage distillation, reconstructs coordinated full-body motion in real time. A pretrained motion encoder maps this motion to a compact latent representation. SP-GMT, a general motion tracker, then integrates the explicit sparse targets with this implicit motion prior for whole-body control. Experiments in simulation and on a real humanoid robot demonstrate improved motion tracking, whole-body coordination, and loco-manipulation success over existing approaches.

METHOD

One interface. Three complementary modules.

01 / SP-VLA

Predict sparse intent

Egocentric RGB, language, and robot state produce five-point pose sequences and finger targets. Asynchronous action chunks keep execution responsive.

02 / SP-MGM

Recover coordinated motion

A compressed motion generator uses offline supervised fine-tuning and rollout-aware distillation to follow sparse targets with temporal consistency.

03 / SP-GMT

Track intent and motion

A motion encoder supplies a compact latent prior. The tracker combines it with explicit sparse poses to generate body joint commands.

MOTION TRACKING & GENERATION

What makes the sparse-pose interface work?

SP-GMT ablations show that explicit sparse targets and an implicit motion prior provide complementary information for global tracking and local motion fidelity. SP-MGM ablations show that rollout-aware distillation improves sparse-pose adherence, while temporal regularization suppresses high-frequency jitter.
